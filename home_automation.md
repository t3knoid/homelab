# 🏡 Home Automation Overview

This document provides an overview of the home automation software and hardware currently deployed in the home. These systems enable automated, conditional actions and provide both local and remote control of the devices they manage.

It is intended to give Home Assistant users context about the existing ecosystem before integrating these devices into Home Assistant.

---

## 🛡️ Security Systems

### 🔔 Ring Security

Platform: Ring
Subscription: Professional Alarm Monitoring

Ring provides comprehensive security coverage throughout the home. Outdoor cameras monitor the perimeter, while indoor cameras cover the main living areas. Additional sensors and auxiliary devices enhance safety and situational awareness.

Devices in use:

- Smart locks
- Smart lights
- Motion sensors
- Door and window sensors
- Smoke and CO₂ detectors
- Ring Chimes for live motion notifications
- Mailbox sensor


### 🎥 Swann Security

Platform: Swann Security
System Type: Local NVR

Swann cameras are installed around the perimeter of the home. Video recordings are stored locally on an NVR. Cameras are configured to detect motion and send alerts.

## 💡 Lighting & Power Control

### 🔌 Tapo by TP‑Link

Platform: TP‑Link Tapo
Formerly branded as Kasa, Tapo controls TP‑Link smart devices including light switches and power outlets throughout the home.

### 💡 Cync by GE

Platform: GE Cync
Used in locations where light switches are wired without neutrals, making them ideal for older wiring configurations.

### 🔌 Topgreener

Platform: Topgreener
A small number of smart outlets use Topgreener hardware, offering functionality similar to TP‑Link smart outlets.

---

## 🌡️ Climate & Environmental Control

### 🌡️ Mysa

Platform: Mysa
Mysa thermostats control 240V baseboard heaters, enabling zoned heating and smart scheduling.

### 🌬️ Carro

Platform: Carro Smart Fans
Carro provides smart control of ceiling fans, including scheduling and automated orchestration.
For example, fan direction automatically changes based on outdoor temperature.

### 🌡️ YoLink

Platform: YoLink
YoLink sensors provide temperature and humidity monitoring inside the home and in outbuildings.
They also monitor refrigerators and freezers and include water leak sensors placed in high‑risk areas such as:

• Under sinks
• Utility room
• Crawlspace


### 🔥 Smartlife

Platform: Smart Life (tuya.com in Bing)
Used to control heaters in the garage and greenhouse, while also providing real‑time temperature monitoring in those areas.

---

## ❄️ Appliances & Miscellaneous Control

### ❄️ SmartHQ

Platform: GE SmartHQ
Used for smart control and automation of air conditioners.

### 🚗 MyQ

Platform: MyQ
Provides remote control and automation of the garage door.

### 🧺 SmartThings

Platform: Samsung SmartThings
Used to control Samsung appliances, including:

• Washer
• Dryer
• Refrigerator
